Background

Crochet has been my hobby for several years now. These are three dolls I crochet for my nephews and niece back in 2024. I took these photos over the course of their creation. I didn't give them names myself since I wanted the kids to name them themselves; I'd have to check in with my sister to see what the kids named them. I have a habit of crocheting dolls and the like for my friends and family around christmas time.
