Canvas Instructions

  • at least one video with native HTML5 controls and poster image
  • at least one audio element
  • at least one CSS or JS driven animation; for example, scroll reveal parallax, animated SVG, typewrite effect, etc.
  • an interactive media control.
  • ACCESSIBILITY: captions, transcripts, or clear text summary are require for all audio and video
  • Autoplay is only allowed if media is muted (personally, I won't allow autoplay since I have it disabled on my own browsers)
  • Proper commentary in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript explaining what it does and why.
