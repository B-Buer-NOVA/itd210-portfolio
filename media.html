Old Music

There's a lot of simple online music production website out there (BeepBox and Chrome Music Lab Song Maker, for example), and I played around with them a little back in the day. Here's a couple of the pieces I made with Chrome Music lab.

it's a little song

This was a little tune I threw together by overlaying several exported tracks from music lab over each other in audacity since music lab doesn't allow for more than one instrument to be selected. I believe I used the marimba and strings for this one.

This one uses the synth instrument which I really enjoy! I called it half step since I set the note length to be really short.
